\[

## 💬 Core Statement

When you connect a battery,  
you do not "send a current".  
You **break the equilibrium**: \( M \not\equiv F \).

The system responds — instantly.  
We call this response **current**.

Current is not a flow.  
It is a **state of the field at the boundary**,  
arising when the medium and the field are forced into alignment.

### Kirchhoff's Law — as synchronous response
- Sum of currents = 0 — not charge conservation,  
  but **simultaneous reaction** of branches to one disturbance.
- Closed loop — not a path, but a **topological form of the field**.

### Faraday's Law — as restoration of field integrity
- Changing magnetic flux breaks \( M \equiv F \),
- Induced EMF — not "induction", but **act of recovery**.
- \( \mathcal{E} = -\frac{d\Phi_B}{dt} \) — rate of violation → strength of response.

All classical laws — not replaced.
